Brand Guide

Logo Guidelines

JOB HUNTING U

The Job Hunting U logo combines a black running figure facing right with the bold condensed wordmark “JOB HUNTING.” The icon represents momentum, action, and forward motion, while the wordmark delivers clarity and authority. Only the “U” is set in blue to create a memorable brand accent and signal the final, outcome-focused step in the customer journey.

This system is designed to stay legible across digital, print, and promotional assets while remaining high-impact in single-colour and full-colour applications.

Logo Policy

Approved usage

Allowed

  • Use the logo on website headers, social graphics, proposals, and marketing materials.
  • Use the full-colour version for digital applications and clear backgrounds.
  • Use the logo in black and blue when presenting on white or off-white backgrounds.
  • Keep sufficient clear space around the mark for readability and visual impact.

Not Allowed

  • Do not stretch, distort, rotate, flip, or crop the logo.
  • Do not change the colour balance, swap the blue accent, or alter the icon shape.
  • Do not reduce the logo below minimum size or place it on noisy or low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Do not use the mark for unrelated materials or in ways that imply a different brand position.

Brand Personality

Direct, In Motion, Outcome-First

The Job Hunting U palette reflects a brand personality that is direct, in motion, and outcome-first. The colour system is built to feel confident, efficient, and action-oriented: black carries authority and clarity, the blue adds momentum and trust, and the off-white creates space for focus and calm. Together, these tones support a brand that moves quickly, communicates clearly, and keeps the customer’s results at the centre of every interaction.

Colours

Colour Policy

Allowed

  • Use the full palette across the website, print collateral, and social touchpoints.
  • Use black for headlines, text, and the core mark to establish contrast and authority.
  • Use blue for links, CTAs, hover states, and small emphasis details.
  • Use off-white as the primary background to keep content crisp, calm, and premium.

Not Allowed

  • Do not introduce alternate shades, tints, or colour variations outside the approved palette.
  • Do not apply blue as a replacement for body text or black as a background for long-form reading.
  • Do not swap the function of each colour in a way that weakens hierarchy.
  • Do not use the palette in a way that reduces contrast or creates a flat, inconsistent brand experience.
